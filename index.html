Mathematical Modeling of Martian Atmospheric $\text{CO}_2$ Cryo-Sorption

Coupled non-equilibrium thermodynamics governing gas-solid adsorption kinetics, Langmuir-Freundlich multi-site isotherms, and non-isothermal freeze limits inside nanoporous Zeolite 13X.

01. Coupled Energy & Mass Balance Navier-Stokes / Heat

The continuous sorption of low-pressure ($6\text{–}10\,\text{mbar}$) Martian atmosphere across a fixed bed packed with synthetic zeolite pellets is governed by coupled 1D axial dispersion and non-isothermal enthalpy conservation:

$$\rho_g \epsilon_b C_p \frac{\partial T}{\partial t} + \dot{m} C_p \frac{\partial T}{\partial z} = k_{\text{eff}} \frac{\partial^2 T}{\partial z^2} + (1-\epsilon_b)\rho_s \Delta H_{\text{ads}} \frac{\partial q}{\partial t} - \frac{4 U}{D_i}(T - T_{\text{wall}})$$

Where $\epsilon_b$ is the interstitial void fraction, $\Delta H_{\text{ads}}$ is the exothermic isosteric heat of adsorption ($\sim 36.4\,\text{kJ/mol}$ for $\text{CO}_2$ on Zeolite 13X), $q$ is specific loading, and $U$ represents overall duct thermal conductance.

Boundary Condition: $T(0, t) = T_{\text{ambient}}$, with heat dissipation dominated by radiative Martian background $Q_{\text{rad}} = \epsilon \sigma (T_{\text{duct}}^4 - T_{\text{sky}}^4)$.
02. Microscopic Sorption & Freeze Limits Sips Isotherm

Adsorption capacity is quantified using the temperature-dependent dual-site Sips isotherm, bounded by the Clausius-Clapeyron dry-ice solid-vapor sublimation phase transition boundary:

$$q^*(P, T) = q_m \frac{\left(b_0 \exp\left[\frac{-\Delta H_{\text{ads}}}{R T}\right] P_{\text{CO}_2}\right)^{1/n}}{1 + \left(b_0 \exp\left[\frac{-\Delta H_{\text{ads}}}{R T}\right] P_{\text{CO}_2}\right)^{1/n}}, \quad P_{\text{sub}}(T) = P_0 \exp\left[\frac{-\Delta H_{\text{sub}}}{R}\left(\frac{1}{T} - \frac{1}{T_0}\right)\right]$$

If local micro-channel vapor pressure $P_{\text{CO}_2}$ exceeds $P_{\text{sub}}(T)$ when $T < 149.2\,\text{K}$, instantaneous desublimation (phase transformation from gas directly to crystalline solid dry-ice) occurs, obstructing the $0.9\,\text{nm}$ cage apertures of the faujasite lattice.

Critical Threshold: $\Delta T_{\text{sub}} = T_{\text{bed}} - T_{\text{frost}} \ge 12.5\,\text{K}$ required to guarantee anti-clogging margin.
03. InSituSieve State Observer Inversion Nonlinear EKF

Because embedded micro-thermocouples cannot penetrate the dense zeolite core without inducing seal failure, the auditor executes a continuous 10 Hz Extended Kalman Filter observing the $(\dot{m}, T)$ phase gradient:

$$\mathcal{H}_{\text{freeze}}(\dot{m}, T) = \frac{\nabla \dot{m}}{\dot{m}_{\text{nom}}} \cdot \text{sign}\left(\frac{\partial T}{\partial t}\right) + \kappa \cdot \max\left(0, \frac{T_{\text{sublimation}} - T}{\sigma_T}\right)$$

When $\mathcal{H}_{\text{freeze}} > 1.0$, the software classifies the condition not as a nominal cold night inlet drop, but as an impending duct freeze-off.
